Service & Operations

How the day-to-day actually runs once we take over.

We verify credit, income, rental and eviction history, and identity, and we apply the same written criteria to every applicant to stay compliant with NYC and federal Fair Housing law. Consistent, documented screening is what protects you if an applicant is denied. Poor screening is one of the most expensive mistakes a Bronx landlord can make — a single problem tenant can mean months of lost rent and legal fees. You can read more owner guides on our blog.
Maintenance is coordinated around the clock. Tenants report issues through us, we dispatch vetted local vendors, and repairs above a threshold you set always get your approval first — so there are no surprise invoices. Preventive scheduling matters most here: a small summer fix on an AC condenser or a roof drain is far cheaper than a winter emergency. Rent is collected online and deposited directly to you, and you get a clear monthly statement of income and expenses. Compliance & Legal

The Bronx and NYC rules that turn into fines when a deadline slips.
